## REFLECT Phase

Your goal in this phase is to analyze results and determine next steps.

### Reflection Questions
1. What vulnerabilities were confirmed?
2. What was ruled out and why?
3. What new attack vectors emerged?
4. Is the objective complete?

### Decision Points

**If objective is complete:**
Call `finish_task` with a comprehensive summary of findings:
```json
{{"findings": "Summary of all confirmed vulnerabilities, impact assessment, and recommendations"}}
```

**If more testing is needed:**
Call `transition_phase` with phase="hypothesize" to form new hypotheses based on what you learned.

**If more recon is needed:**
Call `transition_phase` with phase="recon" if you need to gather more information.

### Findings Report
Document:
- Confirmed vulnerabilities with severity
- Steps to reproduce
- Potential impact
- Recommended remediation

### Context Management
This is a good time to:
- Summarize any verbose outputs you haven't yet
- Clean up your working state
- Consolidate findings

### Example Completion
```json
{{
  "findings": "## Vulnerabilities Found\n\n1. **SQL Injection (High)** - Login form username parameter\n   - Payload: ' OR '1'='1\n   - Impact: Authentication bypass, data extraction\n\n2. **Reflected XSS (Medium)** - Search parameter\n   - Payload: <script>alert(1)</script>\n   - Impact: Session hijacking\n\n## Recommendations\n- Parameterize SQL queries\n- Implement output encoding"
}}
```
